# Aditya's Tech Report — 29 Aug 2026

Model access became a contract weapon, open weights came with new fine print, and a quiet Saturday still shipped a major frontend release.

## 1. OpenAI will cut Cursor off after the SpaceX acquisition

**Tag:** shift
**Why it matters.** Change-of-control clauses are now a live risk for any startup whose product is a wrapper around someone else's models.

OpenAI said it will wind down the contract that supplies its models to Cursor, with a shutoff dated 12 November 2026. The post argues SpaceX — Cursor's new owner — cannot be trusted to stay inside OpenAI's terms, citing past Twitter/X contract issues and sworn testimony that xAI violated those terms. Developers get the maximum notice the custom agreement allows. Cursor can keep shipping on other models; the story is the precedent, not the IDE.

Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/our-decision-on-cursor-following-its-acquisition-by-spacex/) · [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/openai-end-partnership-with-spacexs-cursor-2026-08-29/) · [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49486172)

## 2. htmx 4.0 is out

**Tag:** launch
**Why it matters.** The HTML-over-the-wire camp just took a breaking-change release instead of another 2.x patch.

Released 28 August. Inheritance is now explicit (`:inherited`), events were renamed to a `htmx:phase:action` scheme, and `localStorage` history caching is gone in favor of refetch-on-back. Internals moved from XHR to `fetch`. Morph swaps, `<hx-partial>`, and a cluster of extensions (`hx-sse`, `hx-ws`, `hx-live`) are the actual new surface. There is an upgrade checker. If you copied attributes down the tree by accident, 4.0 will show you.

Sources: [htmx 4.0 announcement](https://four.htmx.org/announcements/2026-08-28-htmx-4.0.0-is-released) · [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49478178)

## 3. Tencent's Hy4 preview — 770B MoE, open weights, coding-first

**Tag:** launch
**Why it matters.** Another hyperscaler just put a giant coding model on the open-weight shelf, a day after the GLM-5.3 license got stricter.

Reuters reports a preview of Hy4: mixture-of-experts, 770 billion parameters, 49 billion active per request, aimed at software engineering, research, and financial analysis, with planned hooks into CodeBuddy and WorkBuddy. It also showed up near the top of Product Hunt today. Treat the parameter counts as vendor-claimed until independent runs land.

Sources: [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/chinas-tencent-releases-new-open-source-ai-model-coding-research-tasks-2026-08-28/) · [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/)

## 4. GLM-5.3 weights are public — MIT is not

**Tag:** shift
**Why it matters.** "Open weights" and "you may host this at hyperscaler scale" are no longer the same sentence.

Z.ai put GLM-5.3 on Hugging Face after a two-week safety hold. GLM-5.2 was MIT; 5.3 uses a custom license that requires companies with more than $10B trailing-twelve-month revenue to pass Z.ai's security review before hosting the model or derivatives commercially. The lab is selling the model's CyberGym score (84.5%, self-reported) as the reason. Anyone already routing it through an aggregator is in a different bucket than anyone serving the weights themselves.

Sources: [The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/zai-glm-weights-license/) · [Z.ai blog](https://z.ai/blog/glm-5.3) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/zai-org/GLM-5.3)

## 5. Samsung showed processing-in-memory at Hot Chips

**Tag:** news
**Why it matters.** If HBM keeps being the bottleneck, moving compute onto the memory die is the unglamorous path that actually ships.

Chips and Cheese's Hot Chips write-up covers Samsung's PIM work — putting compute next to DRAM to cut the data-movement tax that LLM inference pays for. This is not a product drop you can buy on Monday, but it is the hardware conversation that sits under every "we need more tokens per watt" slide. Worth the comments if you care about serving cost rather than model names.

Sources: [Chips and Cheese](https://chipsandcheese.com/p/hot-chips-2026-samsungs-processing) · [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49487341)

## 6. A CLI that boots a virtual iPhone on Apple's own hypervisor

**Tag:** launch
**Why it matters.** iOS testing still assumes a GUI simulator. Someone wired Apple's Virtualization.framework to a command line instead.

`vphone-cli` boots a virtual iPhone using the same framework Apple ships for VMs on macOS. It is a Show-HN-shaped tool, not an Apple product, and the interesting question in the thread is how much of the simulator/Xcode loop this actually replaces versus how much it just proves the hypervisor will tolerate it. If you do CI on Apple silicon, read the repo before you rewrite a pipeline.

Sources: [GitHub](https://github.com/Lakr233/vphone-cli) · [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49485267)

## 7. GUIs should be fully keyboard-driven

**Tag:** take
**Why it matters.** The highest-ranked essay on HN today is not a model card. It is a complaint that pointing devices made software slower to operate.

ckardaris argues for interfaces that are complete from the keyboard — not vim-cosplay overlay shortcuts, but first-class command surfaces. 800+ points says the audience still feels this daily in Electron apps and design tools. Counterpoint from the thread: accessibility and discoverability die when everything is a chord. Still the right Saturday read if you ship an editor.

Sources: [Essay](https://ckardaris.com/blog/2026/08/28/keyboard-driven-guis.html) · [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49479837)

## 8. The rumour of a bug is now enough to find the exploit

**Tag:** take
**Why it matters.** Disclosure culture leaked into a world where an LLM can turn a vague hint into a working PoC.

Anil Madhavapeddy notes that once a class of bug is even rumoured, the search space is small enough that someone — or some model — will produce the exploit before a patch exists. That sits next to GLM-5.3's own vulnerability-discovery marketing. If your disclosure policy still assumes quiet weeks between rumour and weapon, it is out of date.

Sources: [Note](https://anil.recoil.org/notes/rumour-is-the-exploit) · [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49480466)
